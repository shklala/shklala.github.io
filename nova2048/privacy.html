Nova 2048 – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 (v2)

Nova 2048 ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what data the game and its advertising partner handle.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (score, level, coins, settings, achievements) is stored locally on your device. Apart from the optional leaderboard described below, it is not sent to us and there are no accounts.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and app-usage and device information to deliver and measure ads, and to show personalised ads where you consent. In regions where consent is required (EEA, UK) the game shows Google's consent form. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See Google's advertising policy and Google's privacy policy.

Online leaderboard

If you choose a nickname, the game sends that nickname, a random player ID created on your device (not linked to your identity), and your best scores to our leaderboard server so they can be shown to other players. You can play without setting a nickname. To have your leaderboard entries removed, email us with your nickname.

In-app purchases

Purchases (removing ads, coin packs) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google Play tells the game only which product you bought.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your scores and achievements are shared with Google Play Games Services under Google's privacy policy. Signing in is optional.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous gameplay events (for example, which mode was played or where players stop) so we can improve the game. These events are not used to identify you.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
